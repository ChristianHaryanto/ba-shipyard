Customers frequently ask

How does Charity 128 ensure transparency in its operations?


Chari is committed to transparency. We regularly publish financial reports detailing how donations are utilized. Our donors have access to a comprehensive breakdown of expenditures, ensuring complete transparency in every aspect of our operations.

Can I choose which project my donation goes to?


Absolutely. Chari offers donors the flexibility to allocate their contributions to specific projects of their choice. Whether it's education, healthcare, disaster relief, or other initiatives, you have the option to direct your donation to a cause that resonates with you.

How does Charity 128 select and prioritize projects?


Project selection is guided by careful assessment of community needs, collaboration with local partners, and alignment with Chari's mission. We prioritize projects based on their potential for sustainable impact and the urgency of addressing critical needs.

Can I volunteer with Charity 128?


Yes, we welcome volunteers! Chari offers various opportunities for individuals to contribute their time and skills. Visit our website or contact us directly to explore current volunteer openings and discover how you can make a hands-on impact.

How does Charity 128 measure the success of its projects?


Chari employs rigorous monitoring and evaluation processes to assess the effectiveness of our projects. We track key performance indicators, gather community feedback, and conduct regular assessments to ensure that our initiatives are achieving their intended impact.

How does Chari handle emergency relief efforts?


Chari has a dedicated Emergency Response Team that is mobilized swiftly in times of crisis. Through pre-established partnerships and strategic planning, we ensure efficient and effective delivery of emergency relief, including food, shelter, medical aid, and other essentials.

Core Values

At Charity 128, we prioritize transparency, integrity, and inclusivity. These values guide our actions as we work tirelessly to bridge the gap between those in need and those willing to help.
