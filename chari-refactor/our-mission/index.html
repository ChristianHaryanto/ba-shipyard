Our Mission

We are more than a charity donation organization; we are a force fueled by compassion, committed to uplifting communities and transforming lives around the world.

The mission of our organization

At Charity 128, we prioritize transparency, integrity, and inclusivity. These values guide our actions as we work tirelessly to bridge the gap between those in need and those willing to help.

Education for All

Chari is dedicated to ensuring that every child has access to quality education.

Health and Wellness

Our commitment to health and wellness extends across borders.

Disaster Relief

In times of crisis, Chari responds swiftly to provide emergency relief.

Community Development

Chari invests in sustainable community development projects to create positive change.

Chari's Mission: Nurturing Compassion, Transforming Lives

Chari strives to bridge the gap between generosity and those in need, fostering a global community driven by empathy and a shared commitment to making a difference.


Compassion as a Catalyst

At the core of our mission is a deep well of compassion. We believe in the inherent goodness of humanity and the power of kindness to create meaningful, lasting change.


Empowering Communities

We invest in education, healthcare, economic development, and environmental sustainability, striving to create self-reliant and resilient communities that can shape their own destinies.

Join us in the journey to empower and transform lives

Together, we can make a lasting impact through Chari's commitment to kindness and social responsibility.
